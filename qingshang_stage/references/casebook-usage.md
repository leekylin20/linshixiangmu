# Human Dynamics Casebook Usage

The `human-dynamics-casebook` knowledge base contains raw interpersonal and relationship materials. Some files include PUA, cold reading, dominance, recovery, seduction, and manipulation content.

Use the materials as case samples only.

## Use As

- Mechanism evidence.
- Historical examples of persuasion and manipulation patterns.
- Risk examples for self-protection.
- Business analogy material.
- Source material for extracting high-level models.

## Do Not Use As

- Executable scripts.
- Dating playbooks.
- Manipulation instructions.
- Coercive sales methods.
- Emotional abuse tactics.

## Safe Extraction Pattern

For any source passage:

1. Name the tactic neutrally.
2. Explain the underlying mechanism.
3. Identify the risk or ethical boundary.
4. Translate only the safe principle to business.
5. State what must not be copied.

Example:

- Source tactic: intermittent reward in attraction.
- Mechanism: variable feedback increases attention and uncertainty.
- Risk: can become emotional manipulation.
- Business-safe translation: stage product education and benefits clearly without deceptive scarcity.
- Do not copy: deliberately destabilizing a person's emotions to gain control.
