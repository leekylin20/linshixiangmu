# Golden Spatial Anchor: Denim Live Room

Use this example direction as the visual anchor for `denim-live-room`.

The image should first read as a real, clean, premium women's standing fashion live room, not as an offline boutique fitting room and not as an equipment-heavy livestream setup corner.

Core spatial keywords:

- modern women's fashion live room
- clean premium clothing live space
- realistic standing live camera
- full-body jeans display
- controlled room depth
- clothing rack / shelves / curtain / soft interior lighting
- host standing naturally in the room
- spacious floor depth
- not cluttered
- not equipment-heavy

Golden direction:

The room is a natural women's clothing livestream space with a controlled, premium interior. The host stands naturally in the room and fully displays the jeans. The floor has visible depth from the camera foreground to the host's feet and into the background. The background can include clothing rack, shelves, curtain, and soft interior lighting, but these elements support the room depth and clothing display instead of turning the image into a store fitting photo.

Live-commerce identity should be present but subtle. Choose only 2-3 natural signals, such as a wireless lavalier microphone, natural product explanation gesture, a side rack with next jeans waiting to introduce, one small low-density denim sample shelf, or a subtle fit reference card. Do not show all signals at once, and do not expose filming or lighting equipment in the frame.

Avoid:

- equipment-heavy live room
- cluttered operation corner
- large selling boards
- oversized size charts
- live table blocking legs
- warehouse-like stock area
- messy folded jeans piles
- props dominating composition
- over-explained livestream setup
- artificial demo corner

Acceptance:

After removing UI text, the scene should still feel like a realistic standing women's fashion live room with spacious floor depth, natural host posture, full-body jeans display, and clean premium clothing-room atmosphere.
