# Live Room Structure Rules

Every female fashion live-room uses:

> background + midground host zone + side support zone + simple foreground.

## Background

Can include:

- Background wall.
- Wall panels.
- Curtain.
- Wood veneer wall.
- Brand wall.
- Cabinet.
- Wall art.
- Window scene.
- Arch.
- Grid.
- Background screen.
- Low-density rack.

Requirements:

- Not too messy.
- Not too bright.
- Not stealing product priority.
- Not a flat poster backdrop.
- Must retain real indoor spatial relation.

## Midground

Must include:

- Host standing or trial zone.
- Product display zone.
- Walking/try-on zone.
- Host feet placement.
- Camera center focus.

Midground must remain clear. Do not block it with furniture, plants, racks, signs, or foreground objects.

## Side Support Zone

May include:

- Backup hanger.
- Low-density try-on item.
- Simple clothing rack.
- Small side cabinet.
- Low-density shoe/bag display.
- Soft furnishing.
- Floor lamp.
- Single chair.

The side zone supports the live-room feeling. It must not become cluttered or warehouse-like.

## Real Use Feeling

The room may be clean and premium, but it must feel used as a real live-room:

- Side backup hanger.
- Low-density try-on items.
- Simple rack.
- Small side cabinet.
- Subtle floor usage.
- Natural light falloff.
- Soft but imperfect lived detail.

Never make the space look like an unmanned boutique showroom or pure display room.
