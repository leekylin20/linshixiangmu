# Anti Posterization Rules

The output must be a real live-room scene.

It must not become:

- Commercial poster.
- Brand advertising image.
- Magazine cover.
- Photo studio portrait.
- Lookbook image.
- Showroom render.
- Pure boutique display.
- Flat product detail page.

## Space Priority

The space must be a real room with usable scale.

Avoid:

- Flat background.
- One-wall composition.
- Over-decorated set.
- Purely atmospheric interior.
- Unreal AI showroom.

## Host Priority

The host must be in a live-selling state:

- Explaining.
- Touching product details.
- Natural friendly expression.
- Not hard posing.

## Product Priority

Product remains the first subject.

Room and host serve product explanation.
