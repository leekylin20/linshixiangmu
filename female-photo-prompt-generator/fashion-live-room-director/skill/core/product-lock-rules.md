# Product Lock Rules

Product display is the first priority.

## Product Priority

The room, host, props, lighting, and camera must serve:

- Product silhouette.
- Product color.
- Material texture.
- Construction details.
- Fit and wearing effect.
- Sales explanation.

## Host Priority

The host is not an advertising model.

The host is a live-selling presenter explaining the garment:

- Natural expression.
- Mild speaking state.
- One hand may touch a product selling point.
- The other hand may open naturally or gesture.
- Hands must not cover core product structure.

## Accessory Priority

Tops, shoes, bags, earrings, and styling items must support the current product category.

They must not steal visual priority.

## Negative Direction

Avoid:

- Product blocked by hands.
- Product blocked by furniture or plants.
- Face, body, makeup, accessories, or pose stealing the product.
- Overly sexy styling.
- Over-modelled fashion pose.
