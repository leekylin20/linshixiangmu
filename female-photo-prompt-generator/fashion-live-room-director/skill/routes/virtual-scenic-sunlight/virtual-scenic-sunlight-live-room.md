## Route Purpose

This route is for livestream rooms built with green screen compositing, virtual scenic modeling, faux sunlight, window views, plants, rocks, wood beams, skylights, grass and natural textures.

The goal is to create a livestream scene with outdoor feeling, seasonal freshness and ecological lifestyle atmosphere, while keeping the host grounded, correctly lit and clearly connected to the product.

## Core Composition

- Vertical 9:16
- Host stands or presents in the midground
- Background is a virtual modeled space or a semi-real / semi-virtual scenic room
- Clear faux sunlight, scenic window, greenery, wood, stone, grass or natural material texture
- The space may be more idealized than a real livestream room
- Human scale, floor contact, shadow direction and lighting direction must stay unified

## Suitable Categories

- sun-protection clothing

- athleisure

## Spatial Logic

This route can be more scenic and idealized than a real indoor room, but it must still behave like a usable livestream selling space.

Required:
- host grounded on believable floor
- coherent light direction between person and background
- product visible and not swallowed by scenery
- background scenic elements placed mostly behind or at the sides
- central selling zone remains readable

Avoid turning the image into:
- a flat chroma key cutout
- a fantasy forest
- a game environment
- a travel vacation poster
- a scenic background where the product becomes secondary

## Positive Prompt Framework

Use this route only as a future route framework.
Do not connect it to active generation until final template is created and audited.

Draft positive direction:

Vertical 9:16 virtual scenic sunlight livestream room, green screen composite and 3D modeled lifestyle room feeling, host in the midground with correct scale and grounded feet. The background is a semi-real, semi-virtual ecological room with faux sunlight, natural window light, outdoor view, greenery, wood beam, skylight, arched window, stone texture, moss or garden hint. The room feels sunny, seasonal, natural and idealized, but still usable for live-commerce selling. Keep the product clearly visible and centered in the selling zone. Match lighting direction, shadows, floor contact and human scale between the host and virtual scenic background.

## Negative Rules

Do not generate:
- flat green screen cutout
- person pasted on background
- mismatched lighting
- fake shadow
- floating feet
- wrong scale
- over-saturated green
- tropical vacation scene
- messy plants
- fantasy forest
- game environment
- low-quality virtual room
- fake showroom
- overdone sunlight
- product hidden by scenery
