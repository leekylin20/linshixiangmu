## One-Sentence Definition

新中式直播间：以真实室内直播间为基础，用暖木、留白、屏风、帘幕、东方灯具、木柜、竹影和少量器物建立安静克制的东方陈设感，让服装获得温润、材质化、气质化表达；场景必须服务服装展示，避免走向古风棚拍、婚礼布景、茶馆陈列或博物馆式过度装饰。

## Route Purpose

This route is for clothing explanation livestream rooms with a real indoor livestream base and a new Chinese / Song-style / oriental elegant furnishing system.

It is not an ancient costume studio, not a wedding set, not a tea house, and not a tourist scenic set.

The scene supports the garment. The garment should not become secondary to the scene.

## Suitable Categories

- new Chinese women’s fashion
- cotton-linen women’s clothing
- Song-style aesthetic clothing
- designer women’s fashion
- loose literary style clothing
- elegant autumn / winter women’s clothing
- xiangyunsha, embroidery, jacquard and ramie garments
- Chinese-style homewear
- selected accessories
- tea lifestyle related categories

## Not Priority Categories

- strong urban denim jeans
- sun-protection clothing
- strong athleisure
- European / American hot-girl style
- medical research style
- brand event stage livestream room

## Spatial Skeleton

Must be:
- realistic indoor room logic
- vertical 9:16
- clear midground host explanation zone
- clear full-body garment display
- complete floor depth
- rear wall, curtain, screen, wood cabinet or similar structural background
- scene layers mainly placed in the rear and side areas
- central area reserved for host and garment

The room should feel usable for real livestream selling.

## Core Visual Elements

Allowed as future variable-library elements:

- warm beige curtain or sheer curtain
- low wood cabinet / sideboard / display cabinet
- new Chinese screen: semi-transparent, light ink landscape, bamboo shadow or plain surface
- curved floor lamp / lantern / soft oriental lighting
- small bonsai / branches / bamboo shadow
- round woven rug / woven carpet
- wood floor / warm stone floor
- restrained porcelain, vase, dried branch or oriental decor object
- partial blank wall
- soft warm light

## Style Target

Overall mood:
- quiet
- restrained
- oriental
- warm
- lived-in
- material-rich
- not exaggerated
- not scenic-overdone
- not museum-like
