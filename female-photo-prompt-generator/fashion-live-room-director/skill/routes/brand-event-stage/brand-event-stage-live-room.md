# 品牌活动舞台型直播间 route

draft route

## Route Purpose

This route is for brand event livestream rooms where the main visual goal is brand trust, event atmosphere and official campaign presence, rather than full-body garment fitting display.

Suitable for:
- beauty brand event
- skincare brand day
- fragrance launch
- jewelry event livestream
- super brand day
- official flagship livestream
- celebrity guest livestream
- expert host livestream
- new product launch livestream
- multi-host activity livestream

Not suitable for:
- denim jeans try-on
- dress full-body display
- sun-protection clothing try-on
- linen women’s clothing
- any fashion category that requires clear full-body garment silhouette, leg line, hemline or outfit proportion

## Spatial Composition

Use brand facade center-stage composition.

Core layout:
- top brand facade or campaign header area
- central stage-like host and guest zone
- front or right-side product counter
- clean brand wall or architectural background
- strong official event atmosphere
- clear mobile livestream UI-safe layering
- bottom area reserved for comments and shopping UI

Visual structure:
- top: brand identity / campaign title / official event atmosphere
- middle: host, guest, expert or celebrity presentation
- side/front: product counter and display
- bottom: stage floor, clean base, no core information

## Design Logic

This composition sells brand trust first, product second.

It should feel like:
- official brand event
- flagship live square
- luxury counter livestream
- pop-up store stage
- brand launch event
- mall atrium event booth
- campaign live room

It should not feel like:
- ordinary home live room
- offline fitting room
- fashion try-on room
- full-body outfit display room
- low-price shelf selling room
- cluttered retail store
- random studio background

## Camera and Perspective

Use front-facing livestream stage camera.
Natural phone/live camera perspective.
Stable vertical 9:16 composition.
Avoid extreme wide angle, high angle, low angle or fashion editorial camera.

The space can be more frontal and symmetrical than fashion try-on rooms, because this route serves brand event identity and multi-person presentation.

## Product Counter Logic

Product counter is required for small products.

Suitable counter types:
- clean campaign counter
- branded color block counter without real logo
- cosmetic display counter
- jewelry tray counter
- fragrance plinth
- skincare product table

Counter function:
- anchors the commercial focus
- supports small products visually
- connects product display with livestream shopping action
- keeps the event from becoming pure stage show

## People Logic

Allow:
- one host
- two hosts
- host + brand expert
- host + guest
- host + celebrity-style guest

People should look like official livestream presenters.
No over-fashion editorial posing.
No exaggerated model pose.
No sexy styling.
No random crowd.

## UI Safety Logic

This route must respect mobile livestream UI zones.

Top area can carry atmosphere, brand facade, campaign identity, but avoid dense small text.
Middle area should keep host and product visible.
Right side can align with product counter or product information area.
Bottom area should stay clean, using stage floor or base only.

## Positive Prompt Framework

Use this route only as future route framework.
Do not connect it to active generation until final template is created.

Draft positive direction:

Vertical 9:16 official brand event livestream room, brand facade center-stage composition, front-facing mobile live camera view. The room looks like a premium flagship brand livestream event, not a fashion try-on room. Use a clean architectural brand wall, campaign stage feeling, central host and guest presentation zone, and a product counter on the front or right side. The product counter anchors small products and creates a clear selling focus. The composition builds brand trust, official event atmosphere and live-commerce clarity. Use refined materials, brand-color inspired palette, integrated architectural lighting, clean stage floor, and mobile livestream UI-safe layering. ## Negative Rules

- women’s fashion try-on room
- full-body clothing fitting room
- denim display room
- dress try-on room
- ordinary home live room
- offline boutique fitting room
- dense retail shelves
- warehouse stock area
